What’s inside

What else it can do

Medications — in her words

You set a reminder in plain words: blood pressure pill, after breakfast. The bot reminds her, and her “Done ✓” shows up on your side. A missed one makes no noise — just a gray dot in the history.

Brothers and sisters — one shared screen

Everyone sees the same thing. Someone already reached mom — the rest get: “mom’s been talked to today, no need to call twice”.

The month’s history

A month calendar: honey marks, rare cherry days with her words, and a summary — 28 calm days in July. No guilt: a missed day doesn’t “break” anything.

Pause — “mom is away”

She’s gone till Saturday — the bot stays quiet, the widget shows a pause, no false alarms. Two taps from either side.

Voice notes and photos come through

Mom recorded a voice note or sent a photo of her pie — you’ll see it in the day card. And the bot always warmly confirms to her that it got through.

What’s not here

Location tracking. We don’t know where mom is — it simply doesn’t exist in the code.

Reading her chats. The bot only sees what mom tapped or wrote to it herself.

Reproach. Mom “hasn’t replied yet”, never “missed it”. No exclamation marks.

Medical claims. No sensors, no diagnoses, no promises of rescue.

Buttons with no way out. Mom writes “stop” to the bot — it goes silent and her data is deleted.

Payments for mom. The subscription is on your side. For her it’s free, forever.

If we break — you’ll know it was us. You won’t be left thinking mom has gone silent.

FAQ

What people ask before installing

I already text my mom every day. Why this?
A call at seven your time is an alarm clock for mom. A “good morning” in the family chat drowns by lunch. And the main thing: when you get busy and don’t write — nobody notices that mom didn’t reply. The bot notices. It’s not instead of calls — it’s so you call because you want to, not because you’re scared.
Does mom need to install or learn anything?
No. Only Telegram, which she already has. You send a link, she taps “Start” — and from then on it’s one big button every morning. You can walk through the whole thing yourself first and see there’s nothing to learn.
Is this tracking my mom?
No. Location doesn’t exist in the product — it’s simply not in the code, and the bot doesn’t read her chats. You see exactly what she tapped or wrote to the bot herself. And she can stop the bot with a single word, at any moment.
What if mom misses a morning? I’ll lose my mind.
First a reminder goes to mom — you won’t even know about it. If there’s no answer by noon, you get one message, no caps lock, no repeats: usually it’s a dead phone or dropped internet. Nobody wakes you at night.
What if the bot itself breaks?
We tell “mom is silent” apart from “the message wasn’t delivered”. If the morning hello didn’t go out because of us — you get an honest “we’re fixing it”, not a false alarm about mom. An iron rule, built in from day one.
Dad, grandma, both parents — can I?
Yes. “Mom, I’m right here” is just a name. Inside, everything is called what you call it at home: dad, grandma, both parents — each with their own schedule. Brothers and sisters join on the family plan.
Mom uses WhatsApp and I’m on Android. What then?
For now — Telegram for mom, iPhone for you. WhatsApp for moms and an Android version are in the works; mark your platform on the waitlist, it directly drives our priorities.
When is the launch?
The closed beta opens in fall 2026, via TestFlight for you. The waitlist goes first.
